Where Australia's wine comes from, and which regions deliver quality

Three things to remember

  • Three inland regions crush more than two-thirds of Australia's wine grapes.
  • Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Gris have surged since 2001, while Cabernet Sauvignon has slipped.
  • Size and price don't line up - Barossa Valley crushes the most, but Mornington Peninsula has the most expensive bottles.
